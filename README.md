## 🌾 The Vision

Imagine having the weather intelligence for your farm, an evidence-backed crop recommendation, an early warning when disease threatens your crop, a complete cultivation timeline, historical weather records, fertilizer and irrigation guidance, market intelligence, farm analytics, and an AI agronomist — all within a single intelligent workspace.

**That is AgriNova AI.**

AgriNova AI is a **precision agriculture intelligence platform** engineered to transform disconnected agricultural signals into actionable, contextual, and increasingly personalized farm decisions. It brings together **machine learning, computer vision, deterministic agronomic engines, historical farm intelligence, weather intelligence, market data, analytics, and generative AI** into one cohesive agricultural operating layer.

Rather than treating every recommendation as an isolated prediction, AgriNova builds a contextual understanding of the farm — its crops, conditions, activities, historical performance, recurring patterns, and environmental signals — and uses that context to support decisions throughout the crop lifecycle.

> **One farm. One intelligence layer. One place to understand what is happening, what comes next, and why.**

## ✨ What AgriNova Brings Together

### 🌱 Intelligent Crop Recommendation
A multi-signal crop recommendation engine evaluates agricultural conditions such as **temperature, humidity, rainfall, soil characteristics, season, and farm context** to surface suitable crops.

The recommendation architecture combines:
- ML-based crop prediction
- Deterministic agronomic scoring
- Season compatibility filtering
- Candidate generation from the agricultural knowledge base
- Confidence-aware ranking
- Explainable recommendation output
- Historical farm-performance personalization

The system is designed with a **dual-path intelligence architecture**, allowing deterministic rules to remain available when an ML model is unavailable.

### 🤖 Aira — AI Agricultural Intelligence
**Aira** acts as the conversational intelligence layer of AgriNova.

Instead of operating as a generic chatbot, Aira receives structured agricultural context assembled from the platform's underlying intelligence engines. This can include:
- Farm and crop information
- Current weather
- Weather history
- Crop timelines and activities
- Disease history
- Fertilizer and irrigation activity
- Farm performance
- Historical insights and trends
- Deterministic recommendation outputs

This architecture allows generative AI to **explain and contextualize grounded intelligence rather than independently inventing agricultural decisions**.

### 🦠 Disease Detection & Crop Health
AgriNova provides a dedicated disease-analysis workflow for identifying potential crop health issues.

It combines:
- Image-based disease analysis infrastructure
- Symptom and knowledge-base matching
- Confidence-aware results
- Disease records and lifecycle tracking
- Treatment and prevention guidance
- Historical recurrence intelligence

Historical disease patterns can also influence future disease analysis, creating a feedback loop between **past observations and current diagnosis**.

### 🌦 Weather Intelligence
Weather is treated as a first-class agricultural signal rather than a standalone forecast widget.

AgriNova provides:
- Current weather conditions
- Multi-day forecasts
- Temperature, rainfall, humidity and related signals
- Weather alerts
- Historical weather logging
- Weather-aware irrigation guidance
- Weather context for crop recommendations
- Climate summaries within analytical reports

Powered by external weather intelligence services, the platform turns environmental conditions into agricultural context.

### 📅 Crop Lifecycle & Timeline
Once a crop is selected, AgriNova follows it beyond the recommendation.

The platform supports:
- Crop planting and lifecycle tracking
- Growth-stage progression
- Stage-specific cultivation tasks
- Daily task management
- Irrigation and fertilizer activities
- Harvest tracking
- Timeline visualization
- Activity history

From **germination to maturity**, the farmer gets a structured operational view of what should happen throughout cultivation.

### 💧 Irrigation Intelligence
The irrigation engine considers crop, soil, growth stage, and environmental conditions to produce water-management guidance.

Recommendations can be adjusted using:
- Crop water requirements
- Soil characteristics
- Growth stage
- Current weather
- Historical farm behavior

Irrigation activities are also logged, allowing future intelligence to incorporate what has actually happened on the farm.

### 🧪 Fertilizer Intelligence
AgriNova provides knowledge-base-driven fertilizer recommendations aligned with the crop and its growth stage.

The system supports:
- Fertilizer recommendations
- Application guidance
- Stage-specific inputs
- Fertilizer activity logging
- Historical input analysis
- Bounded personalization using farm history

This creates a transition from static fertilizer advice toward **context-aware input intelligence**.

### 📈 Farm Analytics & Performance Intelligence
Agricultural activity becomes useful data rather than disappearing into history.

AgriNova aggregates farm events and historical records to surface:
- Crop performance
- Yield trends
- Seasonal patterns
- Input usage patterns
- Disease recurrence
- Farm activity history
- Historical performance signals
- Personalized insights

The platform's deterministic historical intelligence layer extracts measurable signals before they reach the generative AI layer, providing a stronger foundation for grounded reasoning.

### 💹 Market Intelligence
The market workspace brings agricultural market information into the same operational environment.

It provides visibility into:
- Crop market prices
- Market movements
- Price-oriented insights
- Market alerts
- Crop-specific market context

This connects **cultivation intelligence with downstream market awareness**, helping the farmer view production decisions in a broader economic context.

### 📊 Analysis Reports
AgriNova converts agricultural signals into structured analytical views.

Reports can bring together:
- Climate summaries
- Environmental conditions
- Recommendation outcomes
- Suitability scores
- Explanations
- Farm and crop context

The result is a consolidated representation of the reasoning behind an agricultural recommendation rather than a single opaque prediction.

### 🔔 Notifications & Alerts
Important changes should not remain buried inside dashboards.

AgriNova includes notification infrastructure for surfacing relevant events such as:
- Weather alerts
- Market alerts
- Crop activities
- Scheduled tasks
- Agricultural events requiring attention

### 🏡 Multi-Farm Management
The platform is designed around the concept of the **farm as a contextual intelligence boundary**.

Users can:
- Create and manage multiple farms
- Track individual farm locations and soil information
- Maintain crop histories
- Isolate farm-specific intelligence
- Compare and interpret performance over time

Strict farm-level data isolation is maintained throughout the historical intelligence pipeline.

## 🧠 Intelligence Architecture

AgriNova is deliberately structured as more than an LLM wrapper.

```
                    ┌─────────────────────────┐
                    │        AgriNova AI      │
                    │   Agricultural Layer    │
                    └────────────┬────────────┘
                                 │
             ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
             │                   │                   │
      ┌──────▼──────┐    ┌──────▼──────┐    ┌──────▼──────┐
      │ ML / Rules  │    │ Historical  │    │ External    │
      │ Intelligence│    │ Intelligence│    │ Signals     │
      └──────┬──────┘    └──────┬──────┘    └──────┬──────┘
             │                   │                   │
      Crop / Disease      Farm History /       Weather /
      Recommendations      Trends / Yield       Market Data
             │                   │                   │
             └───────────────────┼───────────────────┘
                                 │
                       ┌─────────▼─────────┐
                       │ Unified Context   │
                       │ & Grounding Layer │
                       └─────────┬─────────┘
                                 │
                       ┌─────────▼─────────┐
                       │ Aira AI Agronomist │
                       │ Explanation Layer │
                       └───────────────────┘
```

The core architectural principle is simple:

**Deterministic systems establish the facts. Historical intelligence establishes context. Generative AI communicates the result.**

This separation improves traceability, consistency, personalization, and resilience.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Backend

```bash
cd backend

python -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate

pip install -r requirements.txt

# Seed the agricultural knowledge base
python -m data.seed_knowledge

# Start the API
uvicorn main:app --reload --port 8000
```

### Frontend

```bash
cd frontend

npm install
npm run dev
```

Open **http://localhost:3000** for the application.

Backend API documentation is available through **Swagger UI** at **http://localhost:8000/docs**.

## 🔐 Configuration

Create a `backend/.env` file with the environment configuration required for your deployment, including:

```env
APP_NAME=AgriNova AI
SECRET_KEY=your-secret-key
DATABASE_URL=sqlite+aiosqlite:///./agrinova.db
```

Production deployments can use PostgreSQL and the corresponding asynchronous database driver.

## 🧪 Engineering Philosophy

AgriNova is built around a few principles:

- **Evidence before generation** — AI operates on structured platform context.
- **Deterministic where precision matters** — numerical and rule-based decisions remain bounded.
- **Personalized through history** — farm history informs future recommendations.
- **Modular intelligence** — crop, disease, weather, irrigation, fertilizer and market capabilities remain independently extensible.
- **Farm-level isolation** — historical context is strictly scoped to its originating farm.
- **Graceful degradation** — fallback paths preserve core functionality when specialized intelligence is unavailable.
- **Explainability by design** — recommendations carry context and rationale rather than existing as opaque scores.
