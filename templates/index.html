Why AgriNova?

Designed to support smarter agricultural decisions through climate intelligence, crop suitability analysis and machine learning insights.

🌦 Historical Climate Intelligence

Retrieves historical weather data for the selected location to provide a reliable foundation for crop recommendations.

🌱 Crop Suitability Analysis

Evaluates temperature, rainfall, humidity, soil type and growing season to rank the most suitable crops.

🤖 Machine Learning Predictions

Generates confidence-based crop predictions using a trained machine learning model alongside rule-based analysis.

🧪 Fertilizer Estimation

Calculates fertilizer requirements based on the recommended crop and the farm size entered by the user.
